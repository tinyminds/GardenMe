ClayMe Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 May 2026

ClayMe is an offline pottery-former design app. It helps you create local project files and export STL files for 3D printing.

Information ClayMe stores

ClayMe stores your saved projects on your device. These projects may include measurements, shape choices, stamp text, stamp pixels, and other app settings that you enter.

Information ClayMe does not collect

ClayMe does not require an account, does not collect analytics, does not track your location, and does not sell personal information.

Sharing and exports

When you export or share a project or STL bundle, ClayMe uses Android's standard sharing features. The app or service you choose to share with may receive the exported file and will handle it under its own privacy policy.

Device permissions

ClayMe only uses file access needed to create, preview, and share generated project/export files through Android system storage and sharing flows.

Children's privacy

ClayMe is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated when ClayMe changes. The effective date above will be updated when that happens.
