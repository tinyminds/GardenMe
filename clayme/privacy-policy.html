ClayMe Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 August 2026

ClayMe is an offline pottery-tool design app provided by TinyMinds. It helps you create local project files and export STL and related files for 3D printing and pottery making.

Information ClayMe stores

ClayMe stores your saved projects on your device. These projects may include measurements, shape choices, stamp text, stamp pixels, imported artwork, and other app settings that you enter.

Automatic Android cloud backup is disabled for ClayMe. You can make your own portable backup from the Projects screen using Export all projects.

Information ClayMe does not collect

ClayMe does not require an account, display advertising, collect analytics, track your location, or sell personal information. The app does not request internet access.

Sharing and exports

When you export or share a project or STL bundle, ClayMe uses Android's standard sharing features. The app or service you choose to share with may receive the exported file and will handle it under its own privacy policy.

Device permissions

ClayMe only uses file access needed to create, preview, and share generated project/export files through Android system storage and sharing flows.

Children's privacy

ClayMe is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated when ClayMe changes. The effective date above will be updated when that happens.

Terms

ClayMe's terms and conditions explain the licence and the responsibilities involved in using generated manufacturing files.
